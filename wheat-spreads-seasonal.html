Methodology & interpretation Contract matching, seasonality and FX

Same delivery year

Each comparison pairs March MATIF with March Chicago SRW or March KC HRW of the same delivery year. These are individual delivery contracts, not continuous futures prices.

April through February

Seasons run from April of the year before delivery through February of the delivery year. Year labels refer to the March delivery. All lines share the same seasonal calendar.

Common currency and quantity

US wheat quotes are converted to a per-tonne basis before comparison. Currency conversion uses the latest FX observation on or before the price date, with a maximum lookback of three calendar days. Missing matches remain missing.

Historical reference

Seasonal bands and historical percentiles exclude the current March 2027 contract and the partial 2002 season. Each reference year contributes its same-season-week mean; bands show the median and 25th–75th percentiles where at least three reference years are available. The latest daily level is ranked against those weekly means. The historical-reference selector controls the comparison sample; year checkboxes control the visible overlays.

Settlement comparison

European and US markets settle at different times. Same-date spreads are closing-market comparisons, not simultaneous executable quotes. The charts do not represent fill prices or trading P&L.

US contract history

Chicago SRW and KC HRW use Norgate daily settlements for individual March contracts, matched to the same MATIF delivery year. Norgate documents its futures Close field as the settlement price; its special 9-suffixed cash-session series are an exception and are not used here. Optional CSV imports replace a view only for the current browser session; reloading restores the published history.

Published data & freshness

This page reads a local, published data snapshot. Observation dates and the snapshot build time appear in Data & coverage; reloading the page does not itself collect new prices. MATIF updates use published Euronext settlements; US contracts come from Norgate and currency conversion uses ECB reference rates. Intraday last trades and unpublished settlements are excluded.
