Priors are qualitative anchors for interpretation. Historical numbers are on the panels; no positioning, sizing or trade logic is displayed on this page.

Method. Front-minus-back spread ($/cwt) computed from DAILY pack HE per-contract settles at hog_analysis/data/raw/per_contract/he_outright_ohlcv1d.parquet. Seasonal band uses 25/50/75 percentile across the last 5 prior calendar years' spread values at each day-of-year; the median line uses the last 10 prior years. The current-year overlay is this year's actual spread. Expiry handoff: near-leg last trading day is the 10th business day of the contract month (CME rule); the summary column shows calendar days from the last available settle.

Repo separation. This page reads hog_spreads_seasonal.json only. The exporter is in hog_analysis/build_hog_spreads.py; the 08:00 rebuild chain copies the JSON into this directory. No cross-imports.

Data quality. "awaiting data" appears when a pair has fewer than 3 prior calendar years at a DOY. ASF (2018) is retained in the historical band as a documented outlier — not stripped.
