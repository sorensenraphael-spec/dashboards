Softs — annual balances (industry standard) LIVE ICCO Feb QB · ISO Aug · trade forecasters to early Sep

Cocoa — surplus/deficit, kt (ICCO convention)

The two surpluses have not refilled the 23/24 hole: −489 +75 +~250 (midpoint) leaves the cumulative three-year balance still negative, with stocks-to-grindings only recovering from its 46-year low (~27%). The 26/27 forecast spread is the widest in softs: StoneX +149 kt / BMI +82 / Guan Chong −300 to −400 on El Niño-Harmattan risk — with early Côte d'Ivoire surveys showing below-average cherelle formation and preliminary 26/27 estimates near 1,800 kt (−20%). A 660 kt disagreement between credible forecasters is itself the trade: the market must price the resolution.

Sugar — surplus/deficit, Mt (ISO convention)

The flip is consensus, the size is not: ISO's fresh 26/27 call is a −0.2 Mt deficit after +1.1 Mt in 25/26, but the trade runs much deeper — StoneX −1.7, Datagro −3.17, Green Pool −3.2 — on two stacked drivers: El Niño over India/Thailand and the Brazil ethanol swing (war-priced gasoline pulling cane away from sugar — the direct bridge to the energy dashboard; UNICA already showed mills cutting sugar mix hard). EU 26/27 production seen −19%. Stocks-to-use at ~44% is in 15-year-low territory: a small deficit lands on a thin cushion. Against that: record fund length — the crowded-agreement cell of the matrix below.

Coffee — surplus/deficit, M bags (USDA/trade convention)

25/26: record world production 189.7 M bags (Brazil 71.9 M, +14%) — a surplus year, yet ending stocks only reach ~8 weeks of use: coffee's "record glut" is thinner than cocoa's deficit-era cushion ever was. 26/27: harvested now, large. 27/28: the battleground — biennial down-year ∩ ONI +1.80 ∩ the florada verdict due by 10 Oct. The balance projection is literally being decided on the rainfall maps of Minas this month; both branches pre-registered in the coffee playbook. Certified stocks tell the distortion story meanwhile: arabica certs at 2.5-yr lows inside the record year (tariff rerouting), robusta certs building on Vietnam's 4-yr-high crop.

Synthesis — balance vs positioning

Tight & crowded — the fragile agreements

Corn (US S/U 10.4% ∩ record MM long) · Sugar (15-yr-low S/U, deficit flip ∩ record London long) · Crude/products (−750 mb cumulative ∩ heavy spec length on war). Right on fundamentals, exposed on flow: any relief headline liquidates first, asks questions later. Size entries for the washout, not the thesis.

Tight & clean — the convex setups

Coffee (8-week stocks, florada live, mid-range positioning — verdict lands on clean books) · US distillates as stocks (30-yr lows; spec length concentrated in crude, less in HO/LSGO — verify in Friday COT). This is where the book's convexity budget belongs.

Diverging forecasts — uncertainty premium

Cocoa: a 660 kt spread between credible 26/27 forecasts, cherelle surveys weak, cumulative balance still below the 23/24 hole. The market must travel from one forecast to the other; own the resolution cheaply (options over outrights while vol underprices the disagreement).

Slack — fade rallies, don't chase

Cotton (64% S/U — yet inside the crowded fund cluster: slack + length = the unwind candidate) · Wheat headline (47% US, China-cushioned world — but ex-China lows and Australia's EN cut argue against pressing shorts) · Gasoline (the byproduct glut).

Standing rule carried from the COT framework: read positioning at book level — corn, sugar and cotton fund length is one correlated position wearing three tickers. The matrix's top-left cell is where that position lives.
