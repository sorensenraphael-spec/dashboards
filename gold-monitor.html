Alert triggers — pre-registered

Checked at every weekly refresh. 🔴 = act on the ratio book / hedge overlay, 🟠 = material development.

  • 🔴The floor cracks: quarterly central-bank net purchases falling below ~150t, or a major holder turning seller — the single regime-ending event for the structural bull; everything else is noise by comparison.
  • 🔴The ceiling clears: sustained ETF inflow streak (4+ weeks) with price recovering through the $4,600–5,000 trapped-cost zone without meeting the expected selling — underwater holders converted from sellers to holders; the path to new highs opens mechanically.
  • 🔴Liquidity-event selling: gold down hard on an equity-crash day — confirmation of phase-1 behaviour; the crash-hedge overlay must not assume gold protects on day one (it just demonstrated it doesn't — it's the phase-2 hedge, after the margin calls clear).
  • 🟠Fed pivot in either direction — hike confirmation extends the correction and the ETF bleed; a cut re-arms both metals and the silver frenzy trigger simultaneously.
  • 🟠Shanghai and India premia/discounts flipping — the Eastern physical bid's honest gauge; sustained discounts = the retail floor softening at these price levels.
  • 🟠COMEX MM net above the ~90th percentile since 2010 (computable from the pack; GC COT is in it) — the fast-money layer joining crowded.
  • 🟠Three-week ETF flow streaks either way — the swing constituency's direction; the floor-vs-ceiling fight scored weekly.

What each regime means for the ratio booklive · 14 Sep 2026

Floor-vs-ceiling coil (current, as of 14 Sep): gold ~$4,380 pinned between an ebbing-but-intact sovereign floor and a rehabilitating ETF ceiling — with the ratio at ~67.6, essentially at the long-run mean. No extreme to lean on: the ratio's direction is set mostly by the silver leg's flow state. In this regime the silver monitor is the active instrument and this one is the FOMC-week sanity check. Structures should be driven by carry and the silver regime, not by a directional gold view.

Ceiling-clears regime (🔴 #2 fires): gold trending to new highs with silver's beta amplifying → ratio compresses. The 12 Sep week of $2.1 B PM-ETF inflows on a red tape is the first datapoint that qualifies as buyer-absorption above prior underwater cost — one more streak week and this regime's early-warning bell starts ringing. If the book carries a short-silver leg at that point, the gold breakout is the standing signal that the silver frenzy trigger is next — the January sequence ran that order.

Floor-cracks regime (🔴 #1): the structural bull's end. Both metals reprice lower, silver harder (beta), ratio widens — historically the regime where the ratio revisits 85–100+. The one scenario where a long-ratio structure pays big; least likely near-term given July's roster held even as the pace stepped down to +23t.

Cross-links: the FOMC on 15–16 Sep is this week's regime-mover — a hike-as-priced is neutral; a hold or hike-with-soft-dots re-arms both metals and pulls the ceiling-clears regime forward. The crash-hedge overlay must still treat gold as a phase-2 hedge (phase-1 sold-with-everything behaviour was re-demonstrated this cycle). Umicore carries the PM exposure into the equity sleeve; the shared-climax fact — gold and silver peaking together in January 2026 — is the standing reminder that manias arrive together and unwind at different speeds, which is where the ratio book earns its keep.
