The divergence — sovereigns accumulating while financial holders bleed — is the market's defining structure and precisely why price has churned rather than trended since January.

Alert triggers — pre-registered

Checked at every weekly refresh. 🔴 = act on the ratio book / hedge overlay, 🟠 = material development.

  • 🔴The floor cracks: quarterly central-bank net purchases falling below ~150t, or a major holder turning seller — the single regime-ending event for the structural bull; everything else is noise by comparison.
  • 🔴The ceiling clears: sustained ETF inflow streak (4+ weeks) with price recovering through the $4,600–5,000 trapped-cost zone without meeting the expected selling — underwater holders converted from sellers to holders; the path to new highs opens mechanically.
  • 🔴Liquidity-event selling: gold down hard on an equity-crash day — confirmation of phase-1 behaviour; the crash-hedge overlay must not assume gold protects on day one (it just demonstrated it doesn't — it's the phase-2 hedge, after the margin calls clear).
  • 🟠Fed pivot in either direction — hike confirmation extends the correction and the ETF bleed; a cut re-arms both metals and the silver frenzy trigger simultaneously.
  • 🟠Shanghai and India premia/discounts flipping — the Eastern physical bid's honest gauge; sustained discounts = the retail floor softening at these price levels.
  • 🟠COMEX MM net above the ~90th percentile since 2010 (computable from the pack; GC COT is in it) — the fast-money layer joining crowded.
  • 🟠Three-week ETF flow streaks either way — the swing constituency's direction; the floor-vs-ceiling fight scored weekly.

What each regime means for the ratio book

Floor-vs-ceiling churn (current): gold rangebound between sovereign bids and trapped-seller supply → the ratio's direction is set mostly by the silver leg's flow state. Practical consequence: in this regime the silver monitor is the active instrument and this one is the weekly sanity check. Ratio ~67 near the long-run mean offers no extreme to lean on; structures should be driven by carry and the silver regime, not by a gold view.

Ceiling-clears regime (🔴 #2 fires): gold trending to new highs with silver's beta typically amplifying → ratio compresses. If the book carries a short-silver leg at that point, the gold breakout is the early warning that the silver frenzy trigger is next — the January sequence ran exactly that order.

Floor-cracks regime (🔴 #1): the structural bull's end. Both metals reprice lower, silver harder (beta), ratio widens — historically the regime where the ratio revisits 85–100+. The one scenario where a long-ratio structure pays big; also the least likely near-term given a record 45% of central banks planning additions.

Cross-links: the crash-hedge overlay must treat gold as a phase-2 hedge — the liquidity-rush selloff just demonstrated phase-1 behaviour again (gold is sold with everything else when margin calls hit, then decouples). Umicore carries the PM exposure into the equity sleeve. And the shared-climax fact — gold and silver both peaked in January 2026 — is the standing reminder that the two legs' manias arrive together, but unwind at different speeds: that speed differential is where the ratio book earns its keep.
