Tier 2 — symptoms (it is happening, hours–days)

🔴
Auction failure signature: coupon auction tails ≥2.5–3bp on 10s/30s with dealer take ≥20–25% and bid-to-cover breaking range. One is noise; the combination is the private bid visibly refusing at these prices.
🔴
The correlation flip — the single cleanest tell: equities down and long bonds down (yields up) on the same risk-off days, especially with the dollar softening and gold up. The absorber has become the seller; the hedge asset has become the exit. Three such days in two weeks = regime, not noise.
🔴
Repo eruption: SOFR ≥ IORB +15bp for 3+ days away from quarter/tax dates; SOFR 99th-percentile blowing out; SRF usage >$25bn sustained on non-dates — the fire extinguisher in actual use.
🔴
Basis unwind in motion: swap spreads gapping more negative (−10bp+ in days) with Treasury futures OI collapsing and off-the-run/on-the-run spreads widening — forced deleveraging's exact fingerprint (Mar-2020 pattern).
🔴
Funding-market contagion: FRA-OIS / SOFR-OIS widening, cross-currency basis blowing out (offshore dollar shortage), fails-to-deliver spiking (DTCC weekly).
🟠
Market-depth collapse: top-of-book depth in 10y futures at percentile lows; bid-ask on off-the-runs widening. Liquidity leaves before price does.
🟠
Primary market shutting: IG deals pulled or postponed; concessions spiking; a hyperscaler jumbo delayed = the AI funding loop's weak link showing.
🟠
New-era runs: a major stablecoin depeg or aggregate supply −5% in a month; prime MMF outflow acceleration.
🟠
Policy tells (confirmation, not prediction): ad-hoc bill purchases, emergency SLR tweaks, auction-size cuts, "temporary" facilities with new acronyms. Each one confirms which bridge cracked.

Policy-response watchlist — the bridges being built

  • SLR relief (re-enables bank absorption): rulemaking progress = bullish duration, bearish vol — the one genuine new absorber on the horizon.
  • Bill-tilted issuance ("activist issuance" = stealth easing): watch each QRA's coupon/bill split — the Treasury managing the gap's routing in plain sight.
  • Standing Repo Facility: capacity and stigma. Its use is a symptom; its expansion is a leading policy tell.
  • Fed reserve-management purchases (post-QT bill buying, later 2026): the balance sheet quietly resuming growth — QE that must not be called QE.
  • Stablecoin legislation/adoption: every $100bn of new supply is $100bn of conscripted bill demand.
  • Treasury buybacks: off-the-run support operations — liquidity provision at the long end, size per quarter.
