Energy Playbook — Crude · Diesel · Gasoline

Sixth playbook in the family. Organizing idea: in 2026 the barrel is not the story — the split is. The war rocks crude, the world is running out of diesel, and gasoline has become a byproduct of making it.

2026 in one line LIVE as of 9 Sep 2026

The regime: February's surplus (fastest inventory builds since 2020, Brent under $70) was vaporized in March by the Iran war — the largest supply disruption in oil history: >13 mb/d of exports lost at the peak, Hormuz loadings down from 20+ to ~3.8 mb/d, global supply −10.1 mb/d in a month, dated Brent touching $140. The 17 June US–Iran MOU to reopen the Strait crushed prices to $72; since July the deal has been fraying. This weekend: US and Iran exchanged strikes, Houthis hit Saudi Jazan (400 kb/d), the US struck Iranian tankers, and Iran declared a toll-corridor scheme with Oman for the Strait. Brent ~$99, WTI ~$93 — highest in ~3 months. UAE has left OPEC, removing the group's main spare-capacity anchor.

The tension to trade: physical reality (record global draws, ~5.1 mb/d in Q2) vs. the banks fading it — JPM sees Brent averaging $86 in Q3, $80 in Q4, $78 at year-end on demand destruction. Every headline cycle since June has resolved as: escalation spikes sold, de-escalation dips bought. The market prices a war ending; the war keeps not ending.

Middle distillates — the star
USGC crack ~$93/bbl

All-time record territory. ULSD-vs-WTI printed $102.20 on 17 Aug — first time ever above $100 (old record: $89, Oct 2022; normal range $15–40). NWE gasoil crack ~$78 (peak ~$95), Singapore ~$72. Structural shortfall, "early innings" per the trade.

Gasoline — the byproduct
Cracks lagging

Driving season over, winter-RVP switch mid-Sep, and refiners worldwide maximizing distillate yield at gasoline's expense. Early-2026 saw 12 straight weeks of US builds to 5-yr seasonal highs. Gasoline weakness is the shadow of diesel strength — a relative trade, not an outright one.

Bull / bear ledger

Bull (complex, distillate-led)

  • Record global inventory draws; OECD on-land stocks fell ~146M bbl in a single month at the war's peak
  • US distillates at ~30-year seasonal lows into heating season + harvest
  • EU double deficit: diesel lowest since 2022 and gas storage ~50% — fuel-switching bid both ways
  • No spare refining kit; every closure since 2020 is now a bid on cracks
  • Hormuz still restricted; each escalation re-prices the whole curve in hours
  • OPEC cohesion broken (UAE exit) — spare capacity less deliverable than quoted

Bear / normalization

  • A durable Hormuz reopening returns >10 mb/d of flow capability — the single biggest bearish binary in any market you trade
  • Demand destruction is real: JPM's case rests on it; China showed imports can be cut hard
  • $90+ cracks are their own cure — every refinery on earth is running for diesel; supply response compounds
  • El Niño warm-winter tilt in the US removes the cold-snap kicker from HO
  • Gasoline already oversupplied — the byproduct glut caps total refinery economics eventually
  • SPR / IEA coordinated releases remain a policy option at $100+
