Alert triggers — pre-registered

  • 🟠Fed pivot headline in either direction — the macro leg that both metals are currently trading; hike confirmation extends the unwind, a cut re-arms the frenzy trigger.
  • 🟠Solar/industrial demand revisions (Silver Institute, PV installs) — the structural deficit leg under the speculation; quarterly check, not weekly.

What each regime means for the ratio booklive · 14 Sep 2026

Frenzy regime (🔴 composite fired): flow overwhelms fundamentals; the silver leg gaps against anything short it and the ratio compresses violently regardless of gold. Playbook: treat as a flow event with a lifespan (weeks), not a thesis change — size down or hedge the exposed leg via listed options per your gap-risk rule, and do not fight the first wave. The Jan 2026 episode compressed the ratio to 45.6 — beyond every fundamental model.

Unwind regime — late stage (current, Sep 14): MM net at +12.6k (Sep 8) is half the Aug-25 peak; the 🟠 three-week-liquidation trigger has now fired. Ratio mean-reverting through the mid-60s (~67.6). The gauge to watch: whether next Friday's COT extends the streak into a fourth down-print — historically the "unowned" readings that follow full unwinds have been the low-risk entry points for whichever ratio structure the book wants next. FOMC Sep 16 is the binary: hike-confirmation extends the regime toward that entry zone, while a dovish surprise re-arms the frenzy trigger overnight. Standing risk: over-staying a short-silver bias into the re-ignition, because the narrative infrastructure (triple-digit targets, deficit story, elevated lease rates, intact retail community) never dismantled.

Dormant regime: positioning mid-range, chatter at baseline, premia normal — the ratio trades on fundamentals (industrial demand, rates, gold's monetary bid) and the book can run its structures without a flow overlay. Silver Eagle premia at 7–9% and SLV flows near zero point at a partial dormancy read overlaid on the active unwind — layered signal, not conflicting.
