Alert triggers — pre-registered

Checked at every weekly refresh. 🔴 = act on the ratio book, 🟠 = material development.

  • 🔴Retail re-ignition composite: any two of — retail-forum silver mention volume spiking to multiples of baseline · coin/bar dealer premiums jumping (silver eagle premia >25–30% over spot) · large sudden SLV/PSLV inflows · silver up >10% in a week on no macro news. This is the short-silver-leg emergency signal: flow events overwhelm ratio fundamentals for weeks.
  • 🔴Physical squeeze mechanics: silver lease rates spiking + EFP (futures−London) blowout + COMEX registered inventory draw — the 2021-style plumbing stress that precedes disorderly moves; hit twice in recent years.
  • 🔴Spec net long above ~90th percentile since 2010 (computable once the data pack is loaded; interim proxy: net long >45–50k contracts or >35% of OI) — futures crowding at the historically dangerous end.
  • 🟠Three consecutive weeks of net-long liquidation — the current unwind completing; historically where the "unowned" buy-zone reads have appeared.
  • 🟠Ratio crossing the 60 long-run mean from above, or back above 80 — the mean-reversion waypoints for the book's structure decisions.
  • 🟠Fed pivot headline in either direction — the macro leg that both metals are currently trading; hike confirmation extends the unwind, a cut re-arms the frenzy trigger.
  • 🟠Solar/industrial demand revisions (Silver Institute, PV installs) — the structural deficit leg under the speculation; quarterly check, not weekly.
